# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

Roles may be staffed by individuals or by teams. A single person may cover more than one role when appropriate, and specialists should be engaged as needed for the project scope.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers turn customer needs into usable, accessible interaction and visual designs. They help the team validate usability throughout discovery and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research
- Create flows, wireframes, prototypes, and final interaction or visual designs
- Define usability and accessibility expectations
- Validate designs with users and stakeholders
- Maintain design guidance and reusable patterns

### How they interact with other roles
- Work with the Product Manager on customer needs, scope, and prioritization
- Partner with Developers on feasibility and implementation details
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility acceptance criteria
- Share findings with stakeholders and incorporate feedback

---

## Technical Leads / Software Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide software architecture and engineering decisions. They align implementation with maintainability, security, operational, and performance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Own or facilitate technical design and trade-off decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration needs
- Promote code quality, testability, and engineering standards
- Review critical designs and implementation approaches
- Support estimates and sequencing of complex work

### How they interact with other roles
- Work with the Project Manager on sequencing, risks, and cross-team dependencies
- Help the Product Manager understand technical constraints and scope trade-offs
- Mentor Developers and participate in design and code reviews
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on testability and critical test scenarios
- Partner with Security/Privacy and DevOps/SRE specialists on release readiness

---

## Data Analysts / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define and validate how success is measured. They ensure that product decisions and delivery reporting use accurate, relevant evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation and measurement approaches
- Validate event and data quality
- Build reports and analyze success metrics and customer behavior
- Explain findings, limitations, and recommended actions
- Help establish baselines and targets

### How they interact with other roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to translate outcomes into measurable indicators
- Coordinate with Developers on events, data pipelines, and implementation
- Work with QA/Testing to validate tracking and reporting
- Provide the Project Manager with progress, impact, and trend information

---

## Security / Privacy Specialists

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Specialists identify threat, compliance, and privacy risks. They help the team design and release safe, responsible solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and implementations for security and privacy risks
- Recommend controls and mitigations
- Support threat modeling and privacy-impact assessments
- Define security and compliance validation needs
- Coordinate security incidents and escalation with the appropriate incident process

### How they interact with other roles
- Work with Technical Leads and Developers to embed safeguards
- Help QA/Testing include relevant security, privacy, and compliance checks
- Provide the Product Manager with risk-informed scope and trade-off information
- Support the Project Manager with approvals, risk tracking, and escalations

---

## DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Site Reliability Engineers improve delivery automation and operational readiness. They help make releases repeatable, observable, and recoverable.

### Responsibilities
- Support CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Maintain environments, infrastructure, capacity, and observability
- Establish monitoring, alerting, rollback, and incident practices
- Improve reliability and reduce operational toil
- Help prepare and verify releases

### How they interact with other roles
- Coordinate with Developers and Technical Leads on implementation and delivery pipelines
- Work with QA/Testing on test environments and release verification
- Help the Project Manager plan deployment windows and dependencies
- Coordinate with Customer Support/Success on operational handoffs and incidents

---

## Customer Support / Customer Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Customer Success representatives bring customer experience and operational feedback into planning and delivery. They prepare customer-facing teams for change and help close the loop after release.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer problems and improvement opportunities
- Review support impacts, help content, and customer communication needs
- Prepare support and success teams for launches
- Represent customer scenarios during planning and acceptance work
- Triage customer impact and coordinate follow-up after release

### How they interact with other roles
- Inform the Product Manager's priorities with customer evidence and feedback
- Help the Project Manager plan communications and launch readiness
- Supply representative scenarios and feedback to QA/Testing
- Coordinate incident escalation and triage with DevOps/SRE and Developers

---

## Role Collaboration and Handoffs

- The **Project Manager** coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, decisions, and communication.
- The **Product Manager** owns outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures customer and business value.
- The **Technical Lead** and **Developers** establish feasible implementation approaches and deliver the solution.
- The **UX/UI Designer** ensures that customer and usability needs shape the solution.
- The **Data Analyst** connects success metrics to instrumentation and evidence.
- The **Security/Privacy Specialist** integrates risk and compliance considerations throughout the lifecycle.
- The **DevOps/SRE** specialist makes delivery and operations reliable and observable.
- **Customer Support/Success** carries customer feedback into planning and prepares teams for launch and operation.
- **QA/Testing** and stakeholders validate acceptance criteria and provide review or approval at appropriate decision points.

These roles should be assigned based on project needs. One person may cover multiple roles, and specialists should become explicitly accountable for their areas when the project creates relevant risk, complexity, or operational impact.
